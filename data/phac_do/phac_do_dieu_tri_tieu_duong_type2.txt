NGUYEN TAC QUAN LY TIEU DUONG TYPE 2 TONG QUAT

Quan ly benh tieu duong type 2 thuong tap trung vao 3 tru cot chinh: kiem soat
duong huyet, dieu chinh loi song, va theo doi bien chung dinh ky.

Ve kiem soat duong huyet, benh nhan thuong duoc theo doi chi so HbA1c dinh ky
moi 3-6 thang de danh gia muc do kiem soat duong huyet trong thoi gian dai,
thay vi chi dua vao duong huyet do tuc thoi. Muc tieu HbA1c thuong duoc ca
nhan hoa tuy theo do tuoi, benh nen kem theo, va thoi gian mac benh.

Ve dieu chinh loi song, cac khuyen nghi pho bien bao gom: duy tri che do an
can bang giam tinh bot don gian, tang cuong van dong the chat deu dan, va
kiem soat can nang. Day la nen tang truoc khi can nhac dieu chinh thuoc.

Ve dung thuoc, metformin thuong la lua chon dau tay cho da so benh nhan tieu
duong type 2 neu khong co chong chi dinh. Khi don tri lieu khong du hieu
qua, bac si co the can nhac phoi hop them thuoc uong khac hoac insulin tuy
tinh trang cu the cua tung benh nhan.

Benh nhan co tien su nhap vien nhieu lan hoac co diem so nguy co tai nhap
cao (dua tren cac yeu to nhu so lan nhap vien truoc do, so thuoc dang dung,
va thoi gian nam vien keo dai) can duoc chu y theo doi sat hon sau khi xuat
vien, bao gom tai kham som va giao duc benh nhan ve dau hieu canh bao can
quay lai benh vien ngay.

[Luu y: day la noi dung minh hoa duoc bien soan cho muc dich do an hoc tap,
khong thay the tu van y khoa chuyen nghiep. Moi quyet dinh dieu tri thuc te
phai dua tren huong dan cua co so y te va bac si dieu tri.]
